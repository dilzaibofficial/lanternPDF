# Domain shortlist: .com only, first year at most PKR 4,000

Checked on **2026-10-19** (registry lookups between about 01:00 and 01:20 UTC). Nothing was bought. Availability and prices can change at any moment, so re-check right before you pay (commands at the end).

## Short answer

**Best pick: `lanternpdf.com`.** Next: `compasspdf.com`, then `meadowpdf.com`.

Why LanternPDF comes first:

- Anyone can say it, hear it once and spell it. It is two plain English words.
- A lantern lights up what is in front of you. That fits one editor plus many tools, and it is easy to draw as a logo in the indigo and teal colours.
- It ends in "pdf", so the topic is clear from the name. In web search I found **no existing PDF product with this exact name**, so people searching for the brand would not land on someone else.
- The registry shows it unregistered as .com, and also as .net, .org and .app.
- At Porkbun it costs US$11.08, which is about **PKR 3,066** (PKR 934 under your limit). The registry price rises on 1 November (about US$0.71 more); even after that it should stay under PKR 4,000.

What I cannot promise: that any domain will rank on Google. Rankings come from the quality of the pages, speed, trust and links, not from the name. A good name helps people remember and search for you. It is not scored as a ranking factor below.

Things that change these numbers:

1. **On 2026-11-01 the .com registry price rises 7%**, from $10.26 to $10.97 a year. This was reported by Domain Name Wire (2026-04-23), Realtime Register (2026-06-03) and Openprovider (2026-09-09), and says it comes from Verisign's contract with ICANN. I could not open Verisign's own notice (its investor site refused the automated request), so treat the date and figure as widely reported, not read at the source. The ICANN fee of $0.20 a year was read directly from ICANN's fiscal-year 2027 letter.
2. After 1 November, expect registrars to charge about US$0.71 more. Cloudflare would be about $11.17 (PKR 3,091). Porkbun has not published a new price; if it passes the rise on in full it would be about $11.79 (PKR 3,263). That is an estimate, not an announcement.
3. Namecheap's own notice says its regular .com prices become $15.98 for registration and $19.98 for renewal, before the ICANN fee. With the fee that is PKR 4,478 to register (over your PKR 4,000 limit) and PKR 5,585 to renew.
4. The contract lets Verisign raise the registry price up to 7% a year in the next three years. That is a ceiling, not a forecast. Renewals are therefore **not** guaranteed to stay at today's price at any registrar.
5. Porkbun's public API does not say whether the $0.20 ICANN fee is inside the $11.08. Price trackers show Porkbun's ICANN fee as $0.00, which means included. Confirm the total at checkout.
6. Not included anywhere: your bank's or card's own charges and any Pakistani taxes. I could not verify those.

## How the check was done

1. **Availability.** A script (`scripts/seo/rdap-check.mjs`) asked the .com registry operator's own RDAP service (Verisign, `rdap.verisign.com`) about **1,391** plain-English names built from the words in `scripts/seo/domain-candidates.mjs`. Result: 1,071 registered, **320 unregistered**, 0 errors. Full list with timestamps: `docs/seo/evidence/rdap-scan-2026-10-19.json`. The 11 finalists were asked again at 01:20 UTC: `docs/seo/evidence/finalists-rdap-2026-10-19.json`. A test with `google.com` came back registered and a nonsense name came back unregistered.
2. **Other endings.** The same name under .net, .org and .app was asked of each registry's own RDAP server from IANA's official list (published 2026-09-30): `docs/seo/evidence/other-endings-2026-10-19.json`. IANA lists no public server for .io or .co, so those were **not checked**.
3. **Existing use.** One web search per finalist for the name as one word and as two words, plus a search for neighbours. This finds products that already exist. It is not a trademark search.
4. **Prices.** Porkbun's public pricing API, Porkbun's published comparison table, Namecheap's own notice, the ICANN letter, and the exchange-rate API. Links below.

## What I could not verify

- **Registrar checkout.** Registrar search pages need JavaScript, and two registrars refused automated reads. I did not try to get around that. So I cannot show that a registrar will sell each name at the **standard** price rather than listing it as a premium name. "Unregistered at the registry" is the registry's own answer, but the checkout page is the last word. **If the price at checkout is higher than the standard .com price, do not buy.**
- **Trademarks.** I did not search trademark databases (they need a browser session or paid tools). Nothing here is legal clearance. Before you spend on a logo and branding, search the name yourself at the US trademark office (USPTO), the UK office and WIPO, or ask a professional.
- **.io and .co** (no public lookup), social media handles, and app-store names.
- **Anything about Google rankings.** Unknown for every name.
